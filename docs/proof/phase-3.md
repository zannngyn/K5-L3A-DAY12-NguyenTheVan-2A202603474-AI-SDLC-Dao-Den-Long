## Design Assumptions & Ambiguities

- **Full-lantern interact check under Step 2-before-Step-5 tick order**: Because Step 2 subtracts `1/60` fuel per tick before Step 5 runs `interact`, a lit lantern starting at `fuel = 100` (`6000` units) drops to `5999` units (`99.983`, displaying `100.0`) on tick 1 before interact runs. Treating a lit lantern as full when `fuelUnitsToDisplay(fuel_units) >= 100` (i.e. `fuel_units >= 5997`, within 3 ticks of full) ensures the Ticket-03 Section 1 rule *"A full lantern (used = 0) is not selectable, emits no event, gives no score"* is reachable both in tick-1 scenarios and immediately after lighting/refueling, while `fuel = 99.9` (`5994` units) remains refuelable.

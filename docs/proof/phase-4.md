## Memory Investigation Note (Soak Test)

Initial 10-night soak runs showed process RSS growing from `94.5 MB` to `127.2 MB` (`1.34x`), even though V8 `heapUsed` stayed flat at `11.5–15.1 MB`. Root-cause profiling with `tsx --no-turbofan` and per-night RSS logging isolated the `+25 MB` native RSS spike to V8 TurboFan compiling the monolithic 1,110-line `Sim.step()` method around Night 5, plus per-tick temporary array/tuple allocations (`safeLanterns`, `keptMotes`, `keptDrifters`, `remaining`, `resolvePlayerHorizontal`, `isShadeBlocked`, `facing`). Decomposing `Sim.step()` into 12 private phase methods and eliminating per-tick heap allocations brought 10-night soak RSS down to `94.7 MB -> 102.7 MB` (`1.084x`) and cut Night 3 18,000-tick headless runtime from `869 ms` to `480 ms` with byte-identical outputs.

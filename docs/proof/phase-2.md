## Changed / Removed v1.0 Tests & Reasons

Every v1.0 test change corresponds directly to a breaking v1.0 -> v1.1 spec change in `.swarm/specs/design-phase2.md` section 7:

1. `tests/sim.test.ts` — `game.contract.json tuning equals src/sim/tuning.ts TUNING`: updated expected `contract_version` from `"1.0"` to `"1.1"` (Contract v1.1).
2. `tests/sim.test.ts` — `normalizes move vectors longer than 1 and rotates by cam_yaw_deg`: replaced instant 1-tick top-speed assertion with 6-tick (`0.1 s`) linear acceleration/deceleration and `6.0 m/s` top speed assertions (v1.1 acceleration model + standardized `player_speed = 6.0`).
3. `tests/sim.test.ts` — `acceptance: player (0,0,0), mote (0,0,5), moving +Z yields mote_picked at tick 28`: updated expected pickup tick from `28` to `43` and pickup score from `10` to `0` (v1.1 `player_speed = 6.0`, `0.1 s` acceleration ramp, `pickup_radius = 1.0`, and score awarded on deposit rather than pickup). 4. `tests/sim.test.ts` — `supports partial deposit, rising-edge interact, lighting lantern, and scoring`: updated lantern cost `5 -> 10`, interact radius `3.0 -> 2.0`, deposit score `+10/mote`, and combined with safe-zone push-out verification (`<= 30` ticks, no damage).
